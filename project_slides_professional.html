Executive Summary

PetCare Management System

A desktop platform for pet stores and animal care businesses, consolidating pet intake, health tracking, inventory, sales, memberships, and reporting into a secure local workspace.

PythonPySide6SQLiteLocal-first
Problem

Operational challenges

Issues

  • Data fragmented across tools
  • Manual reports delay decisions
  • Service and stock are disconnected
  • Customer retention is hard to manage

Impact

  • Slow operations
  • Lower service quality
  • Higher operational risk
  • Lower customer satisfaction
Goals

Objectives

Centralize

Unify relevant business functions into one app.

Automate

Reduce repeated manual work and improve operational rhythm.

Control

Monitor stock, services, customers, and payments more clearly.

PetCare is a practical local business management platform that unifies pet operations with stock, billing, services, and security in one desktop environment.
