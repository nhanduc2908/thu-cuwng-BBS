# PetCare Management System

## Slide 1 — Title
PetCare Management System
Pet Store Management Software

### Speaker note
Introduce the system as a desktop-based management platform for pet stores and animal care businesses.

## Slide 2 — Business Problem
- Pet business data is scattered across many tools.
- Inventory, customers, schedules, and billing are not connected.
- Manual process increases errors and slows service.

### Speaker note
This project solves a real operational challenge: disconnected data and inefficient daily workflows.

## Slide 3 — Objectives
- Centralize pet, customer, inventory, and service data
- Reduce manual work and improve productivity
- Improve customer satisfaction and sales support
- Strengthen security and operational control

### Speaker note
The goal is not only technical implementation but also business efficiency and transparency.

## Slide 4 — Main Features
- Pet profile and intake management
- Inventory and stock control
- Sales and payment management
- Service scheduling and appointments
- Membership and loyalty tracking
- Reporting and alerts

### Speaker note
The system covers the full operational cycle of a pet business, not just one isolated function.

## Slide 5 — Architecture
- UI built with PySide6
- Business logic in modular application layers
- SQLite as local database
- Security and audit controls

### Speaker note
The architecture is modular and suitable for a local-first desktop application.

## Slide 6 — Security
- PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password hashing
- Unique salt per user
- Mandatory password change for first admin login
- Role-based access and audit logs

### Speaker note
Security is designed to protect sensitive data and provide accountability across staff actions.

## Slide 7 — Business Value
- Faster daily operations
- Better inventory visibility
- Stronger customer service
- More structured service delivery
- Better decision-making and reporting

### Speaker note
This system improves operational discipline and creates long-term business value for pet stores.

## Slide 8 — Conclusion
PetCare is a practical, secure, and scalable internal system for pet stores and animal care businesses.
It brings together business operations in one local application and provides a foundation for future expansion.

### Speaker note
End by emphasizing that the system is both technically solid and practically valuable for small and medium pet businesses.
