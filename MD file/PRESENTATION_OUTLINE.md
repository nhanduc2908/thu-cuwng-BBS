# Presentation Outline (16:9)

## Slide 1 — Title
PetCare Management System
Pet Store Management Software

## Slide 2 — Project Overview
- Desktop application for pet store management
- Built with Python, PySide6, and SQLite
- Local-first architecture
- Internal business management workflow

## Slide 3 — Problem Statement
- Business information is fragmented across tools
- Pet, customer, stock, and service data are difficult to track together
- Manual updates cause errors and inefficiency
- Need for centralized data and reporting

## Slide 4 — Objectives
- Integrate all key operations in one platform
- Improve customer service quality
- Strengthen stock control and transaction visibility
- Support faster operational decision-making

## Slide 5 — Main Functional Modules
- Authentication and user management
- Animal management
- Care and health tracking
- Customer management
- Inventory management
- Sales and billing
- Services and appointments
- Membership management
- Reporting and alerts

## Slide 6 — System Architecture
- UI layer: PySide6 interface
- Business logic layer: modules for operations
- Data layer: SQLite database
- Security layer: password hashing, permissions, audit logs

## Slide 7 — Authentication and Security
- Default admin setup on first launch
- Password hashing with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256
- Salt per user
- Mandatory password change on first login
- Login audit logging

## Slide 8 — Pet Management
- Detailed pet profiles
- Intake records and handover documents
- Housing and location tracking
- Image storage and verification
- History and operational records

## Slide 9 — Care and Health
- Care checklist management
- Health observations and history
- Health-related records and reminders
- Staff responsibility tracking
- Better operational monitoring

## Slide 10 — Customer Management
- Customer data management
- Ownership and relationship mapping
- Service and purchase history
- Deposits and balance tracking
- Customer loyalty support

## Slide 11 — Inventory Management
- Product catalog and pricing
- Batch and expiry tracking
- FEFO stock allocation
- Low-stock and near-expiry alerts
- Stock reservation logic

## Slide 12 — Sales and Payment
- Order creation
- Partial payment support
- Stock reservation for pending orders
- Combo and item-level deduction logic
- Cancellation and stock release

## Slide 13 — Service Scheduling
- Appointment booking
- Pet-to-customer linkage
- Staff assignment
- Price estimation before confirmation
- Tracking statuses: pending, active, completed, cancelled, no-show

## Slide 14 — Membership Management
- Membership packages
- Card issuance and bill generation
- Activation and renewal
- Payment schedules
- Loyalty and benefits management

## Slide 15 — Recommendation Engine
- Offline rules-based recommendation
- Pet profile, inventory, and budget-aware suggestions
- Product explanation tags
- Feedback on liked/disliked items
- Better product sales support

## Slide 16 — Reporting and Analytics
- Inventory reports
- Sales reports
- Service reports
- Membership reports
- CSV export and operational monitoring

## Slide 17 — Security and Compliance
- Role-based access control
- Audit trail for critical actions
- Sensitive data protection
- Local database management
- Backup recommendations

## Slide 18 — Key Risks and Limitations
- No MFA or 2FA support
- Local deployment requires secure device access
- No public-facing e-commerce layer
- Recommendation is rules-based, not advanced AI
- Not a legal tax invoice or veterinary diagnostic platform

## Slide 19 — Business Value
- Faster and more accurate operations
- Reduced manual data errors
- Better stock and service control
- Improved customer satisfaction
- More transparent management reporting

## Slide 20 — Competitive Advantages
- Integrated single-platform system
- Low implementation cost
- Fast deployment
- Suitable for small and medium pet businesses
- Flexible to local business workflows

## Slide 21 — Future Development
- Dashboard analytics enhancement
- PDF export and financial reporting
- Payment gateway integration
- More advanced recommendation engine
- Scalability toward larger business operations

## Slide 22 — Conclusion
- PetCare is a practical, scalable, and secure management platform
- It supports full pet business operations in one desktop environment
- It addresses real operational pain points for local pet businesses
- It is a strong foundation for future expansion and modernization
