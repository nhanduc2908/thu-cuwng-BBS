Project Overview

PetCare Management System

A desktop-based business management platform for pet stores and animal care facilities. It integrates pet records, customer intelligence, inventory, sales, services, memberships, and operational reporting into one local-first application.

Python PySide6 SQLite Local-first
Problem

Why this system is needed

Operational pain points

  • Pet, customer, and sales data are scattered across multiple tools.
  • Inventory and service management are hard to track consistently.
  • Staff cannot easily monitor care schedules, bills, and memberships.
  • Manual workflows increase errors and operational delays.

Business need

  • One place to manage all core business data.
  • Faster customer service and operational control.
  • Centralized reporting and alerting.
  • Improved accountability and data consistency.

Authentication

Authentication and user access

  • Application starts with a default admin account.
  • Passwords are stored using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256.
  • Each account has a unique salt.
  • First-time admin login requires a password change.
  • Login attempts and important events are stored in audit logs.

Security

Security design

Implemented

  • PBKDF2 password hashing
  • Random salt per user
  • Mandatory password change on first login
  • Role-based permissions
  • Audit log for critical actions

Additional safeguards

  • Secure local data storage
  • Regular backup policies
  • Restricted account access
  • Operational accountability and traceability
Limitations

Operational and technical constraints

  • Not a public e-commerce or customer portal
  • No payment gateway integration
  • No public QR or online payment flow
  • Recommendation is offline and rules-based
  • Not a legal invoice or veterinary diagnosis platform
