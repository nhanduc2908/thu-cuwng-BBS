Business case

Business case for the PetCare platform

The platform addresses fragmented internal workflows with one local desktop application. Efficiency, retention, and financial gains are evaluation hypotheses; no store pilot measurements are supplied in this report.

Key points

Operational problem

  • Customer, care, stock, reservation, and service work can require separate records and repeated entry.
  • Staff need a consistent workflow for creating, updating, and reviewing operational records.
  • Management measures are only useful when definitions and source periods are agreed.

Implemented response

  • One Windows desktop application groups operational areas around a local SQLite database.
  • Role-aware screens and business rules support intake, care, stock, sales, memberships, and services.
  • Offline recommendations use configured rules; they are not external AI or a payment service.

Assumptions to test

  • Whether a shared record reduces duplicate entry in the selected store workflow.
  • Whether staff can complete defined tasks with fewer corrections or handoffs.
  • Whether customer retention changes against a documented baseline and cohort.

Pilot decision method

Baseline

Record a fixed pre-pilot period, eligible transaction/task counts, staff time sample, and exclusion rules. Confirm the starting data with the store owner.

Comparison

Use the same metric definitions and period length after rollout. Report counts and denominators alongside percentages; do not treat missing records as zero.

Decision gate

Proceed only when required workflows, data quality, backup handling, staff training, and privacy responsibilities have named owners and acceptance criteria.
