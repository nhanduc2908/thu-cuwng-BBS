Performance highlights

Current view

Revenue quality

Revenue is increasingly driven by recurring care plans, memberships, and cross-sell nutrition bundles, improving predictability.

  • More recurring revenue
  • Higher customer lifetime value
  • Improved margin durability

Customer experience

Fast coordination, transparent recommendations, and care follow-up are creating stronger loyalty and easier upsell opportunities.

  • Fewer missed steps
  • More proactive communication
  • Higher trust and referrals

Operations

Operational consistency is improving as workflows consolidate data and reduce repeats across scheduling, records, and stock movement.

  • Clearer task ownership
  • Better stock visibility
  • Faster issue resolution

Recommendation

Decision

Keep focus on customer value

Invest in personalization and care continuity to increase renewals and premium add-ons.

Protect operating discipline

Audit logging, role-based permissions, and workflow governance should remain non-negotiable.

Expand thoughtfully

Scale by branch, service line, and data maturity rather than by feature quantity alone.
