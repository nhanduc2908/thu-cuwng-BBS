Executive summary

PetCare is a local-first Windows desktop application for internal pet-store operations. It brings animal records, care, inventory, sales, memberships, services, and reporting into one PySide6 application backed by SQLite; business impact remains to be measured in a pilot.

Key points

Verified product scope

  • One local desktop application uses SQLite for operational persistence.
  • Modules cover intake, care, health records, customers, stock, orders, memberships, and services.
  • Recommendations use configured offline rules rather than a generative AI provider.

Decision requested

  • Review the current build and confirm the workflows required for a controlled store pilot.
  • Name operational, data-protection, and backup owners before loading live customer records.
  • Agree baseline measures and acceptance criteria before evaluating business impact.

Evidence boundary

  • Automated tests cover selected logic; their results must be rerun and recorded for the submitted revision.
  • HTML demos and sample KPI values are illustrative, not live operating results.
  • No ROI, retention uplift, or productivity gain is claimed without pilot evidence.
