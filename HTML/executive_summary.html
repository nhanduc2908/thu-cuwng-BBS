Executive Summary

PetCare is positioned to become the operational backbone of modern pet service businesses.

The project is centered on a single strategic objective: unify the customer journey, operations, and health decision-support into one system that is simple enough for a boutique pet shop and scalable enough for multi-branch expansion.

Key executive observations

What matters

1. This is a workflow platform, not only a catalog

PetCare solves the core problem of fragmented experiences across care, nutrition, customer tracking, and commercialization.

2. Nutrition is a strategic differentiator

Product matching, age/weight guidance, and care personalization significantly improve trust and conversion opportunities.

3. Operational clarity drives premium pricing

Higher-quality service and better records enable better experience, stronger retention, and more efficient operations.
