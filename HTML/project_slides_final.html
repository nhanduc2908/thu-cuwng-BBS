PetCare project presentation

A concise executive summary of the platform, covering the core operational areas, the design objectives, and the strategic value of the local-first desktop solution for a pet care business.

Desktop app SQLite data Service ops Business value

1. Problem

  • Pet care businesses often split customer, health, stock, and service records across disconnected workflows.
  • Manual handoff leads to repeated entry, missed updates, and slower operational decisions.

2. Product idea

  • A single local desktop application that centralizes customer data, pet records, services, and inventory.
  • Role-aware workflow access makes the system easier to govern and scale.

3. Functional scope

  • Intake, records, health tracking, care operations, memberships, stock, orders, and reports.
  • Support for service scheduling, status-driven workflows, and business review reports.

4. Business impact

  • Improves consistency and speed across routine internal operations.
  • Creates cleaner customer follow-up and stronger staff accountability.
