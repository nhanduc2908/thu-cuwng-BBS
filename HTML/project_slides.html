PetCare project summary

The project is structured around one local-first desktop platform that brings pet intake, owner management, care workflows, inventory, memberships, and business reporting into a single operational environment.

Business context

  • Pet-focused businesses need a consistent view of customer, service, stock, and care records.
  • Operational work is often split across fragmented tools and manual handoffs.

Platform concept

  • One desktop application with structured modules and role-based permissions.
  • Local persistence using SQLite reduces infrastructure complexity for a small or medium business.

Core value

  • Faster operational workflow and clearer team accountability.
  • Better customer follow-up and improved lifecycle tracking for care and service.

Next stage

  • Validate in a pilot environment with measurable baselines.
  • Expand only after using evidence to confirm operational impact and adoption.
