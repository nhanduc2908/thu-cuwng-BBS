PetCare operational workflow

The platform is organized as a local-first stack of operational modules. Users start in an authenticated environment, move through role-based navigation, and then work across intake, care, inventory, service, and reporting flows.

Authentication Role access Service ops Reporting

Core workflow stages

Operational backbone
01

Launch

The application starts and loads the local data store, preparing the operational environment for user tasks.

02

Authenticate

Users sign in with internal credentials. Permissions are assigned to guide the data access and actions available in the app.

03

Choose module

Navigation branches into customer management, pet records, care workflows, services, inventory, shipping, and reporting.

04

Execute and review

Records are updated, scheduled, and followed through with an explicit operational state to support accountability.
