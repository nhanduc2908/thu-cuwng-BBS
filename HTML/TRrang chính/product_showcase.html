01 Key points

◆

Internal customer workflows

  • Maintain customer records and review related activity.
  • Record reservations, deposits, orders, and staff-confirmed payments.
  • Manage memberships as internal records; there is no public signup or automatic renewal.
▣

Store operations

  • Track store-animal intake, inspection, housing, care, and health observations.
  • Manage supplier references, product catalog, batches, stock movements, and alerts.
  • Configure service packages, estimates, staff assignments, and appointment states.
◇

Management and boundaries

  • Review reports generated from available saved records and selected periods.
  • Use offline rules-based suggestions as operational aids, not medical advice.
  • Validate seed data, pricing, backup handling, and role access before a real pilot.
