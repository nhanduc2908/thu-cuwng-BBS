Separate paths

Keep the two record types distinct

Store branch

Store-intake animal

Supplier/import batch → handover photo and staff confirmation → inspection → AVAILABLE, QUARANTINE or TREATMENT. This is a store-operation animal record.

Customer service

Customer-owned pet

Customer → pet data on the customer profile → quote → appointment recording pet name/species. The appointment does not store a pet-profile ID or store-animal ID.

Do not infer or merge these paths. This is a presentation scenario intended to explain the workflow logic, not a claim that every store follows the same exact process or data model.
