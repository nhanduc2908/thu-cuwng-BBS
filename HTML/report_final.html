Project outcome

Summary

Problem addressed

Pet businesses lose operational time, customer trust, and revenue consistency when records, care tasks, and product decisions are fragmented.

Product response

PetCare provides a centralized, local-first platform for pet management, nutrition guidance, memberships, service planning, and operational reporting.

Expected value

Improved retention, more efficient scheduling, stronger care coordination, and easier product recommendation workflows.

Implementation sequence

Roadmap
Phase 1Build core pet, appointment, care, inventory and customer services.
Phase 2Introduce nutrition recommendation, service bundles, and referral behavior.
Phase 3Expand analytics, governance, and branch-aware scheduling.
Phase 4Prepare for advanced scale, reporting, and multi-site operation.

Final recommendation

Decision

Proceed with structured rollout

  • Launch the core workflow foundation first
  • Integrate nutrition and care plans early
  • Use data and feedback to guide expansion

Protect governance

  • Role-based access and audit trails
  • Clear decision ownership
  • Operational controls for data quality

Measure impact

  • Customer retention
  • Repeat purchase rate
  • Operational efficiency and SLA
