Demo storyline

1
Open customer profile

Staff starts with a pet owner record and sees the pet list, age, health status, and recent care notes in one panel.

2
Review personalized care

The app surfaces maintenance tasks, vaccination schedule, nutrition advice, and suggested product categories based on age and lifestyle.

3
Recommend nutrition

Staff can compare food products by target goal such as digestive support, weight management, senior care, or allergy-friendly options.

4
Save / confirm order

A service visit or nutrition order is documented, assigned to a staff member, and tracked through the next action or follow-up step.

Key moments

Customer experience

  • Single screen for pet profile and account history
  • Quick summary of priority care tasks
  • Easy way to explain recommendations in plain language

Operational efficiency

  • Less time switching between spreadsheets and tabs
  • Faster action on new requests and reminders
  • Monthly records organized by customer lifecycle

Commercial value

  • Nutrition product matching improves conversion
  • Care recommendations build trust and retention
  • Dashboards help managers monitor volume and quality
